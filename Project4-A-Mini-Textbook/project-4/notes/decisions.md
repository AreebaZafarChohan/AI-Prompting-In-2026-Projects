# Key Decisions & Rationale

## 1. Topic & Audience
- **Topic:** Newton's Third Law of Motion
- **Audience:** High school students

## 2. Approach Selection
- **Selected:** **Option C — Predict, Test, Explain** (Problem-Solving / Inquiry)
- **Rejected:** **Option B — Force Pairs on Paper / Diagram-Centered**
- **Reason for Rejection:** A diagram-centered approach may feel too dry at the beginning and may be harder to understand before the core concept is intuitive.

## 3. Outline Selection
- **Outlines Considered:**
  1. Minimal Scaffolding — Pure Inquiry
  2. Guided Inquiry — Prediction + Light Visual Support
  3. Story-Driven Inquiry — Prediction + Narrative Thread
- **Selected Outline:** **Outline 2 — Guided Inquiry**
