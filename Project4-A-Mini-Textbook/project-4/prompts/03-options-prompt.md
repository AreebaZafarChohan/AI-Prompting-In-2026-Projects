# Prompt 03: Original Pedagogical Approaches

## Context:
The AI proposed three distinct pedagogical approaches for presenting Newton's Third Law to high school students:

### Option A:
**Everyday Physics — Intuition-First**
- Grounding the law in familiar daily physical experiences before formalization.

### Option B:
**Force Pairs on Paper — Diagram-Centered**
- Visual and schematic-focused approach using interaction diagrams and free-body representations.

### Option C:
**Predict, Test, Explain — Problem-Solving / Inquiry**
- Scaffolding learning around predictions, thought experiments, and resolving misconceptions through guided inquiry.
