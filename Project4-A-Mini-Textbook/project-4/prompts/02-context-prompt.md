# Prompt 02: Context-Rich Prompt

## Prompt Sent:
```text
I am a high school student learning about Newton's Third Law of Motion. Explain it to me clearly at my level, then tell me what is still unclear and what I might misunderstand.
```

## Context & Observations:
- **Audience Specified:** High school student.
- **Direction:** Requests clear level-appropriate explanation paired with explicit identification of common confusions and misunderstandings.
