# Topic Definition

- **Subject:** Physics (Mechanics)
- **Topic:** Newton's Third Law of Motion
- **Core Principle:** For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction ($F_{A \text{ on } B} = -F_{B \text{ on } A}$).
- **Target Audience:** High school students (Introductory Physics / Conceptual Physics).
- **Learning Objectives:**
  1. Understand that forces always occur in matched interaction pairs.
  2. Identify action-reaction force pairs in real-world scenarios.
  3. Recognize that action and reaction forces act on *different* objects, resolving the common misconception about why they do not cancel out to zero net force on a single object.
  4. Relate force pairs to differing accelerations based on Newton's Second Law ($F = ma$).
