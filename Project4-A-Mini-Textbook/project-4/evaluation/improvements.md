# Chapter Improvement Record

This document records the specific, minimal improvements applied to `project-4/chapter/draft.md` based directly on `project-4/evaluation/rubric.md`.

---

## 1. Light Visual Support (Skater & Truck/Mosquito Diagrams)
- **What Changed:** Added clean ASCII/text force interaction diagrams to Section 2 (Skater interaction) and Section 5 (Truck vs. Mosquito collision).
- **Why It Changed:** To visually demonstrate that action-reaction force pairs act on *two different objects* and point in opposite directions, reinforcing the physical concept after predictions.
- **Rubric Item:** *Clarity*, *Age-fit*, and *Checklist: Light Visual Support after Prediction*.

---

## 2. Notation Breakdown & Clarification
- **What Changed:** Added a dedicated beginner-friendly subsection in Section 3 breaking down $\vec{F}_{\text{A on B}}$, $\vec{F}_{\text{B on A}}$, the negative sign ($-$), and the equals sign ($=$).
- **Why It Changed:** High school students frequently struggle with subscript syntax and vector direction notation when introduced without explanation.
- **Rubric Item:** *Clarity* (Rubric Item 1).

---

## 3. Simultaneous Action and Reaction Clarification
- **What Changed:** Added an explicit callout note in Section 3 emphasizing that "action" and "reaction" happen simultaneously as part of a single mutual interaction, rather than in a cause-and-effect sequence.
- **Why It Changed:** The historical phrasing "action and reaction" easily misleads students into believing that one force triggers the other after a delay.
- **Rubric Item:** *Accuracy* (Rubric Item 2).

---

## 4. End-of-Chapter Recap
- **What Changed:** Added Section 7 ("Chapter Recap") summarizing the 4 core takeaways: Forces as interactions, Newton's Third Law definition, why pairs do not cancel, and the distinction between force and acceleration.
- **Why It Changed:** Provides a consolidated reference for quick review before testing.
- **Rubric Item:** *Usefulness for Revision* (Rubric Item 4) and *Checklist: Recap*.
